# Founder forcing questions

Rewritten for this kit. Interest is not demand.

## Demand

- Who already changed their behavior for this? Quote the behavior.
- Has anyone paid, begged for a date, or gotten angry when a prototype broke?
- Waitlists and “that’s interesting” do not count.

## Status quo

- What do they do today (spreadsheet, chat, vendor, ignore)?
- What does that workaround cost them (time, money, risk, embarrassment)?
- If the status quo is “nothing,” the pain may be too weak to move.

## Wedge

- If we could ship only one path this month, which one would be missed?
- What do we refuse so the wedge stays sharp?
- Platform vision is allowed as a later chapter, not as v1 scope.

## Failure patterns to name once

Solution in search of a problem · hypothetical users · waiting for perfect ·
confusing social proof with demand.
