# Iron law — no fix in the investigation

**Why:** mixing diagnosis and patch loses the theory. The next session cannot
tell what was proven vs what was guessed-and-edited.

**Allows:** read-only shell, log capture, reproducing HTTP, notes and scripts
in the scratch/report path.

**Forbids:** editing product files, migrations, “temporary” config in the
repo, restarting services to “see if it helps” as a substitute for a cause.

**After:** the assistant (or FRIDAY) may open a build slice that cites this
report. The report is an input, not a license to skip verify-live.
